# Accessibility Statement

Effective date: 30 October 2026.

## Our commitment
NuroTok and neurodigitalsupport.com are made for people who can be overwhelmed by what they see and hear online, including autistic, ADHD and other neurodivergent people. We want both to be usable by as many people as possible, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on the website and their equivalents in the app.

## What NuroTok offers
- Atkinson Hyperlegible type throughout, designed for readability.
- Light, dark and calm colour themes, with contrast checked for text and controls.
- Reduced motion: NuroTok's own animations, the floating mascot and slideshow advance hold still.
- A screen tint, greyscale shortcut and brightness control in the sensory toolkit.
- Labels for screen readers on every button and chart, including the counts the mascot shows.
- Plain, kind language, with no streak shaming or countdown pressure.
- Large touch targets and support for Android font scaling and tablets.

## Website
The website supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, text resizing up to 200%, a reduced-motion preference and an accessibility menu for text size and contrast.

## Known limitations
- Some third-party content shown in Lite mode and embedded videos is controlled by those services and may not meet these standards.
- App switches recognise apps by their screen structure in English; on other phone languages some switches may not act.
- Older blog posts may have images without full text descriptions; we are adding them.

## Feedback and help
If something is hard to use, tell us and we will help and fix it. Email info@neurodigitalsupport.com with "Accessibility" in the subject, or call +44 7825 354088. We aim to reply within 5 working days. If you are not satisfied with our response in the UK, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS): https://www.equalityadvisoryservice.com/.

## Preparation of this statement
This statement was prepared on 30 October 2026 from our own testing of the app on Android phones and tablets and of the website in current browsers.
