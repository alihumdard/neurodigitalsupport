# NuroTok Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: 30 October 2026.

## Delete your account in the app
Open You > Account > Delete account and confirm. This deletes, from our server, your NuroTok account and everything linked to it: your devices, synced usage (daily totals and reel events), synced settings, family links and referral records. Your sign-in details go with it: your password, your Google or Apple link, friend battles and plan history. It happens straight away; backup copies on our hosting are overwritten within 30 days.

## Stop usage sharing and delete what was sent
Open You > Privacy and switch off Usage sharing. Usage already stored on our server (daily totals and reel events) is deleted at once, and nothing more is sent. Your account stays.

## Request deletion without the app
Email info@neurodigitalsupport.com with subject "NuroTok account/data deletion". You do not need to reinstall the app. Include the account email or sign-in method and whether you want the whole account deleted or only usage data. Never send passwords, verification codes or card details. We may ask for proportionate proof that the account is yours and respond within the legal deadline (normally one month).

## Remove information on your phone
Open You > Privacy. Delete insights removes insights history. Delete all data clears locally stored profiles, settings and histories and restarts onboarding. Repeat on each device. Delete exported backups and shared copies separately.

## Information that may remain
Limited financial, transaction, security or support records may need to remain for legal obligations, disputes or fraud prevention. Records held by Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics), RevenueCat and the app stores follow their own deletion processes; ask us and we will request deletion where we can.

## Family links
Deleting a parent's account ends every link it had. Deleting a child's account ends its link and removes its usage from the parent's view. Either side can also end a link without deleting the account.

## Children and parents
A child can do all of this themselves, from their own phone: switch off usage sharing (which deletes the usage already shared), leave a family link, or delete their account. They do not need the parent's permission to leave a link or delete their own data.

A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child's account or shared usage. Email info@neurodigitalsupport.com with subject "NuroTok account/data deletion", give the child's account email or sign-in method, and say what you want deleted. We may ask for proportionate proof that you have parental responsibility, and where the child is old enough to understand, we take their views into account. Do not send more of the child's details than we ask for.

## Cancel subscriptions separately
Deleting data, an account or the app does not cancel billing. Use https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions or https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
