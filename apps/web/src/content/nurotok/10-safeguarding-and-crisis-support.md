# Safeguarding and Crisis Support

Effective date: 30 October 2026.

## If you are in danger now
NuroTok is not an emergency or crisis service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 in the UK (112 in the EU, 911 in the US) or go to your nearest emergency department.

## Talk to someone now (UK, free)
- Samaritans: call 116 123, any time, or email jo@samaritans.org.
- Shout: text SHOUT to 85258 for 24/7 text support.
- NHS 111: call 111, or 111 online, for urgent mental health help; choose the mental health option.
- Childline (under 19): call 0800 1111 or chat at https://www.childline.org.uk/.
- Papyrus HOPELINE247 (under 35): call 0800 068 4141 or text 88247.
Outside the UK, find local services at https://findahelpline.com/.

## Within NuroTok
NuroTok's AI chat screens messages for signs that someone may be at risk and shows these crisis lines instead of continuing as normal. The AI is not a counsellor and cannot contact anyone for you. Crisis numbers in the app can be updated by us without an app update, so they stay current.

## Children and families
Parent controls are designed to support a conversation, not replace one. A linked child's phone always shows that a parent is linked, and a parent can never read a child's messages, journal or moods through NuroTok. If you are worried that a child is being harmed or is at risk, contact your local authority children's services, or the NSPCC helpline on 0808 800 5000 (help@nspcc.org.uk). In an emergency call 999.

How we protect children who use NuroTok:
- The child's best interests come first. Parent controls only change protective settings (such as reel allowance, focus times, sleep schedule and website blocking); a parent cannot sign the child out, read their profile or see anything shown in other apps.
- Privacy is high by default: usage sharing, analytics and crash reports are off until switched on.
- The child is told: their phone shows that a parent is linked and what the parent can see and change.
- The child can leave a family link from their own phone at any time.
- No ads, no profiling and no selling of information.

**If you are a young person** and feel unsafe, or unhappy about how parent controls are being used, talk to an adult you trust, or contact Childline (0800 1111) for free and in confidence. You can also email us.

## Reporting a concern to us
If you have a safeguarding concern about NuroTok, our website or anyone acting for NeuroDigital Support, email info@neurodigitalsupport.com with "Safeguarding" in the subject or call +44 7825 354088. We treat every report seriously, respond within 1 working day, and refer to the appropriate authority where required. Never include a child's full details in an email unless asked; we will arrange a safer way to share them.

What happens after you report:
- We reply within 1 working day to say we have your report and what happens next.
- If someone may be at immediate risk, we will contact the police or local authority children's services, even before we have all the details.
- We share information only with people who need it to keep someone safe, and keep records of the report securely and confidentially.
- We tell you the outcome where we can, though we may not be able to share everything.

Our email and phone are not monitored around the clock. If a child or anyone else is in danger now, call 999 first.

## Online harms
To report harmful content you have seen on a social platform, use that platform's reporting tools. In the UK, Report Harmful Content (https://reportharmfulcontent.com/) can help with content that platforms have not removed, and the Internet Watch Foundation (https://report.iwf.org.uk/) takes reports of child sexual abuse imagery.
