# NuroTok: Easy Read

Effective date: 31 October 2026.

This page says the main things in our privacy policy and terms, in easy words.
The full pages still count. If they say something different, the full pages are right.

## What is NuroTok?

NuroTok is an app.
It helps you feel calmer on your phone.
It can hide things that upset you.
It can count reels and help you watch fewer.

## What stays on your phone?

Most things stay on your phone.
Your moods and journal stay on your phone.
Your sensory profile stays on your phone.
What the filter sees is never saved.

## What do you send us?

You can make an account. You do not have to.
If you make one, we keep your email and name.

You can switch on usage sharing. It is off at first.
Then we get numbers, like how many reels you watched.
We never get what you watched.

You can switch it off again.
Then we delete those numbers.

## Family

A parent can link a child's phone.
The parent can see the child's numbers.
The parent cannot see messages, moods or journal.

## Who else gets your information?

Some companies help us run NuroTok.
For example, for payments and notifications.
They only get what they need.
We never sell your information.

## Paying

Some parts cost money. This is called Premium.
You pay through the app store.
You can stop paying in the app store.

## NuroTok is not a doctor

NuroTok can help you feel calmer.
It is not medical help.
If you feel unsafe, call 999.
You can call Samaritans for free on 116 123.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app.
Go to You, then Account, then Delete account.
You can also do it on our website.
We then delete your information.

## Your rights

You can ask what we keep about you.
You can ask us to change it or delete it.
Email info@neurodigitalsupport.com.
We will answer within one month.

## Questions

Email info@neurodigitalsupport.com.
You can ask for help with anything on this page.
